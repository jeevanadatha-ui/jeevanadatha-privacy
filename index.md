# Privacy Policy — Jeevanadatha (Rice Mill Operations)

_Last updated: 2026-10-14_

Jeevanadatha is a rice-mill business operations app used by mill owners,
operators and their staff ("you"). This policy explains what data the app
handles and how.

**HOSTING NOTE:** this text must be published at a public URL (e.g.
`https://<your-domain>/privacy`) and that URL pasted into the Play Console
App content → Privacy policy field.

## What the app collects

| Data | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Google account email (sign-in) | To identify the mill account and secure your data | Verified server-side; never shared |
| Name, phone, village of farmers/agents you enter | Business records for your mill | Your mill's encrypted-at-rest cloud database |
| Paddy purchase records (weights, bags, prices, payments) | Operating your mill's procurement ledger | Your mill's cloud database |
| Photos you attach (slips, profiles, vehicles, bills) | Record keeping | Private cloud storage; only your mill account can view them |
| Vehicle numbers, locations of mills/vehicles | Business logistics records | Your mill's cloud database |
| Device location (approximate / precise, only when you use a location feature) | Filling a mill/vehicle address faster | Used in-app for the field you are filling; not tracked in the background |

## What we do NOT do
- No advertising, no selling of data, no third-party analytics or trackers.
- No background location tracking. Location is read only when you use a
  feature that needs it.
- Your data is isolated per mill account — one mill can never see another
  mill's records.

## Data storage & security
- All data is transmitted over HTTPS (TLS).
- Data is stored in a private cloud database scoped to your mill account.
- Photos are stored in private cloud object storage and served via
  short-lived signed URLs.

## Data retention & deletion
- Records are kept for as long as your mill account is active.
- Deleting a record in the app deletes it from your mill's data (photos are
  removed from storage). Deleting your mill account removes all its data.

## Permissions used
- Internet (to sync with your mill's cloud data)
- Camera / photo library (only when you attach a photo)
- Location (only when you use a location field)
- Notifications (to inform you about app events you choose)
