# Account & Data Deletion — Jeevanadatha

_Last updated: 2026-10-14_

You can request deletion of your Jeevanadatha account and all
data associated with it at any time.

## How to request deletion

**In the app** — open Jeevanadatha → Settings → Delete my account,
then confirm. (Also available from your Profile screen.)

OR

**By email** — send a request to
**niteeshlakamsani2001@gmail.com** from the email address you
used to sign in. Subject: "Delete my account". You will receive
a confirmation within 7 days; deletion is completed within 30 days
of confirmation.

## What is deleted on confirmation

- Your mill profile, account settings and crop-season configuration
- All business records: weighbridge entries, truck sheets,
  payments, expenses, sales, invoices, bags, vehicles, farmer
  and agent records
- All photos you attached (slips, profiles, bills) from storage
- Your subscription / trial record

## What is retained, and for how long

After deletion we retain only minimal security / audit logs
(no business content) for up to 30 days, then permanently erase
them. Backups are overwritten on the standard 90-day cycle.
